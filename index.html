System Guide & Vercel Deployment

🚀 Workflow Overview

This application executes the Teacher Reminder & Acknowledgement System CrewAI agentic workflow. The crew reads teacher recipient data from a Google Spreadsheet, composes tailored reminder notices, and logs responses and acknowledgements.

📋 Required Inputs

  • spreadsheet_id: The Google Sheet containing teacher information, email addresses, and acknowledgement columns. Make sure the Google Sheet is shared with your CrewAI service account email.
  • reminder_input: Plain language instructions describing what reminder to send (e.g. deadline, event date, acknowledgement conditions).
